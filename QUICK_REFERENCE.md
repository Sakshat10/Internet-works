## Key Analogies

- **Internet** = Network of networks (NOT just Wi-Fi)
- **Router** = Traffic controller for data
- **DNS** = Internet's phonebook
- **IP Address** = Home address for devices
- **ISP** = Road connecting to highway
- **Packets** = Breaking large box into small packages
- **Server** = Restaurant kitchen
- **Encryption** = Locked diary

## Common Student Questions

**Q: Is Internet the same as Wi-Fi?**
A: No! Wi-Fi is one way to connect to the Internet.

**Q: How fast is the Internet?**
A: Messages travel in milliseconds (thousandths of a second)!

**Q: Can people read my messages?**
A: Not with encryption (lock icon/HTTPS)!

**Q: What is DNS?**
A: Like a phonebook - translates google.com to IP address.

**Q: Why break into packets?**
A: Efficiency, reliability, multiple paths!
